# Code of Conduct

The Mitochondria is a scientific and engineering collaboration. Contributors are expected to create a professional, respectful, evidence-minded environment.

## Expected behavior

- Treat contributors with respect regardless of role, background, experience, or viewpoint.
- Critique ideas, code, evidence, and design decisions without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and clearly distinguish fact, hypothesis, uncertainty, and opinion.
- Respect privacy and never pressure others to disclose personal or health information.
- Give appropriate credit for code, scientific sources, designs, and other contributions.
- Prioritize safety and transparency when discussing health-related behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate humiliation, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, privacy violations, plagiarism, knowingly fabricated evidence, and intentional introduction or concealment of unsafe behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action to protect the project and its contributors.

This code applies in project repositories, review spaces, documentation, demos, and community interactions made on behalf of The Mitochondria.
