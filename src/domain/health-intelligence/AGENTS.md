# Health intelligence rules

- Crew Self evidence (Companion and all four Health pages) is projected read-only by src/domain/health-intelligence/canonical-projection.ts from scalar envelopes, reviewer-accepted unified observations and catalog-validated domain entries; stored records are never rewritten, quality is never inferred, identical readings are charted once, and every in-scope record that cannot be charted is listed with a reason so evidence remains traceable.
- The Medical Report (/mission/health-report) is built deterministically by the pure read models in src/domain/health-intelligence (health-history-report.ts, unified-health-report.ts) for one authorized mission/crew/provenance: reviewer-accepted or human-verified rows only, never mixing real and synthetic, device rows only with declared provenance and canonical scalar metrics, everything else listed as excluded, and no diagnoses, thresholds or inferred trends.
- Unified medical intake stores observations only as versioned envelopes (mitochondria.unified-observation.v1) through src/domain/health-intelligence/observation-store.ts: per-measurement catalog classification, reviewer-accepted rows only route to domain read models, and storage stays encrypted, append-only and offline (no cloud writes).
- Medical Report PDF export captures existing print styles in an isolated browser clone, paginates at report-item boundaries and generates local A4 bytes; fresh caller scope checks before export and after rendering prevent revoked or switched scope downloads without medical-data uploads.
- Scientific Findings & Interpretation (scientific-findings.ts) is a pure read-only layer over the built Unified Health Report: first/last differences only for two distinct comparable time points at the recorded precision, cross-domain context only for signals listed verbatim in data/scientific/nikolas-cross-domain-context.v1.json, never causality, thresholds, diagnoses or fitness-for-duty; widening signal matching requires governed scientific review, not code-side synonyms.
- Structured intake formats (XLSX first sheet, plain JSON, FHIR R4 Observation/Bundle) are parsed on-device by universal-intake-adapters.ts into the same review candidates as CSV: only explicit zoned ISO timestamps (FHIR effective[x], never issued), no unit/date conversion, unmapped codes stay review-only, and repeated metric/time/value rows need explicit acknowledgment.
- Unknown Biomarker Review (unknown-biomarker-review.ts) groups review-only intake rows by normalized label + exact unit; decisions are ground-medical-only, append-only, session-memory and always PENDING_NIKOLAS_REVIEW. Classification may only gain matches from data/scientific/catalog-extensions.v1.json entries that are APPROVED with a named reviewer, decision time and handbook evidence, alias an existing routed P36 metric with its exact unit, and do not conflict; the app never writes that registry.
- The Scientific Health Summary (scientific-health-summary.ts) quotes biology only from the English handbook layers joined by exact catalog ID, reports zero validated interpretations while review is pending, and explains domain-overview vs Medical Intake count differences; screen, PDF and TXT share the same builder.
