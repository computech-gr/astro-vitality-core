# P21.14 — Independent Scientific Review Handoff

This patch prepares an **actual human-review work package** for the project biologist and independent subject-matter reviewers. It does not claim scientific sign-off.

- 63 handbook variables: prioritize the six candidate exact mappings, then five related-not-equivalent mappings, then 52 unmapped variables.
- 16 relationships: review evidence strength, transferability of terrestrial analogs to flight, confounding, incompatible specimens/assays/units, and contradictory evidence.
- Six publications: independently verify methods, results and quantitative claims against the actual full text; do not treat abstract screening or a located URL as full-text verification.

## Reviewer workflow

1. Assign a named reviewer and obtain primary literature. Record stable PMID/DOI and precise page/figure/table/section.
2. For each metric verify biological meaning, specimen, assay, units, sampling timing, confounders and personal-baseline suitability.
3. For each relationship record whether the evidence supports an **educational, non-diagnostic** explanation; distinguish astronaut flight evidence from bed-rest analogs.
4. Record approve_educational_only, request_revision, reject or defer in a separately governed signed review artifact after independent assessment. Do not edit the generated pending packet to fabricate approval.
5. Any clinical or operational rule requires a separate safety and medical governance process; P21.14 does not enable it.

This handoff is deliberately not an automated medical decision engine. The P21.15 baseline freeze must report pending work transparently and must not convert unreviewed relationships into approved rules.
