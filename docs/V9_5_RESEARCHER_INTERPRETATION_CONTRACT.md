# V9.5 Researcher Interpretation Contract

Scope: Bone, Cardiovascular, Immune, Behavioral, plus Unified Health PDF and TXT parity.

1. Shared per-domain view: observed measurements, comparable time points, magnitude and direction, exact unit, evidence IDs, sampling coverage, provenance, quality and limitations.
2. Biological context must come only from existing exact-ID governed handbook mappings, with source and approval status. Bone/Cardiovascular may show pending hypotheses; Immune/Behavioral must not fabricate mappings.
3. Distinguish Ground Medical attestation, intake acceptance and independent scientific interpretation approval.
4. Do not infer abnormality, disease, clinical thresholds, diagnosis, treatment, fitness for duty, causal associations or validated trends from two readings.
5. Cross-domain comparisons require temporally aligned signals, compatible measurement conditions and sufficient data; missing data is never normal/healthy evidence.
6. Ensure module screen and Unified PDF/TXT share the same deterministic interpretation model. No network AI calls in report generation.
7. Preserve append-only histories, encrypted sync and existing authorization. No migrations, uploads, publishing or changes to scientific approvals.
8. Test four modules (with and without data), mixed domains, mismatched units, same-day observations, missing handbook mapping, attestation states, Unicode Greek PDF, responsive layouts and no fabricated scientific claims.
