# P21.7 — Independent Scientific Evidence Retrieval Plan

This stage turns 16 P21.6 appraisal placeholders into 16 explicit, traceable literature retrieval tasks. It **does not perform searches**, establish independent evidence, or approve claims. Search strings are discovery prompts, not citations.

## Human reviewer workflow

1. For each `bhrel-*` relationship, search authoritative NASA HRP reports and indexed primary studies. Record full bibliographic metadata, DOI/PMID or persistent URL, access date, exact study population, method, outcomes, uncertainty, and relevant limitations.
2. Distinguish actual spaceflight evidence from head-down bed rest, terrestrial analogs, animal work, mechanistic hypotheses, and reviews. Do not transfer effects or thresholds between populations without justification.
3. Independently verify sample type, collection timing, assay, units, DXA site versus QCT modality, baseline and confounders. Search contradictory evidence as well as supportive evidence.
4. Reviewer signs and dates decisions only after assessing source-level evidence. A source being retrieved is not a scientific approval. Keep the existing P21.6 appraisal and P21.4 ledger pending until formal review.
5. Never convert handbook author tiers into independently verified evidence; no clinical thresholds, diagnostic claims, automated alerts, causal inference, or operational activation in P21.7.

The JSON plan is a **queue**, not a review result. The verifier checks cross-file references and enforces zero premature approval. New literature must be assessed separately and must not be inserted as fictitious citations.
