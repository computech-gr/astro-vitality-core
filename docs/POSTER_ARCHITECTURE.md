# P20 Poster / Architecture Content

## Headline

**The Mitochondria — Healthy People. Farther Together.**

Open-source, offline-first astronaut health intelligence for long-duration human spaceflight.

## One-line architecture

**Devices & labs → adapter SDK → canonical health model → personal baseline → explainable intelligence → protocol guidance → encrypted offline runtime → governed ground/research console**

## Poster blocks

1. **Challenge** — radiation, altered gravity, isolation/confinement and closed-environment stressors increase the need for crew autonomy.
2. **Monitor** — wearables, medical/lab data, cognition and habitat/radiation telemetry.
3. **Personalize** — immutable pre-flight and phase-aware personal baselines.
4. **Explain** — multi-signal findings with evidence, confidence, uncertainty and confounders.
5. **Act** — bounded protocol guidance, not autonomous diagnosis.
6. **Stay operational offline** — encrypted local event log and store-and-forward queue.
7. **Reconnect safely** — mission/crew authorization, audit trail and de-identified research export.
8. **Open source** — Apache-2.0, reproducible local deployment, synthetic public demo data.

## Safety footer

Prototype for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge. Independent project; no NASA endorsement implied. Not clinically validated. No real astronaut medical data is used in the public repository or demo.
