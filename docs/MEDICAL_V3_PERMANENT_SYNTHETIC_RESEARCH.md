## Controls and accounting

Separate SQL-provisioned owners control budgets, rolling quotas, emergency pause and audited recovery. Ground-medical membership alone never confers administration. Exact mission/crew, source SHA and both original observation IDs/62/66 bpm/timestamps remain mandatory. Caller-authenticated RPCs own admission and serialized pause/dispatch; server-held nonces permit one fetch without retries.

Each request irreversibly reserves USD 0.10. Monthly available budget is budget minus holds minus recorded dedicated-project costs; this deliberately double-counts overlapping costs conservatively. Recorded provider costs, local estimates, holds, attempts, dispatch permits and provider-reported requests are distinct. Unresolved/billing/outage outcomes pause and retain the active hold until owner recovery; rolling quotas replenish without lifetime expiry, monthly budget uses UTC boundaries. Existing 5/user/minute and 20/mission/minute limits, 4096-byte requests, 6144 input-token cap and 1500 output-token cap remain.

Pause commits before denying later dispatch permits. Already-permitted/in-flight requests may transmit, complete and bill after pause; actual provider charges and delayed/external usage cannot be absolutely capped. Recovery never refunds holds or automatically retries. The monthly ledger is an admission budget, not prepaid credit balance or an absolute cash ceiling.
